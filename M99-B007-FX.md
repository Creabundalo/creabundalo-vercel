# M99-B007 — FX

## Goal
Model currencies as bilateral prices embedded in rate differentials, carry, capital flows, policy regimes and global liquidity.

## Architecture additions
- FX_PAIR identity is directional and ordered (base/quote).
- FIXING_REFERENCE and tradable market price are distinct observations.
- CARRY / RATE_DIFFERENTIAL is first-class context.
- NOMINAL and REAL_EFFECTIVE exchange rates are separate state families.
- Intervention / peg / managed-float regime is explicit state.

## Core sensors
- bilateral spot;
- forward/futures points and basis;
- policy/rate differential;
- carry;
- implied/realized volatility;
- options skew;
- CFTC positioning;
- effective exchange rates;
- balance-of-payments / trade context where relevant;
- global liquidity / dollar funding;
- central-bank regime/events;
- meaning world.

## Representative cases
- CHF-2015-SNB-FLOOR-EXIT — regime break/gap.
- GBP-2016-BREXIT — event + repricing.
- EURUSD-2014-2015 — policy divergence.
- USDJPY-CARRY-CYCLES — rate-differential/carry mechanics.
- USD-2021-2022 — broad-dollar tightening regime.

## Batch result
Independent FX domain/source/case fill is DONE.
Detailed options and futures execution routes remain under B004.
