## Case taxonomy
Cases may carry multiple labels:
- LIFECYCLE
- BUBBLE_CANDIDATE
- DISTRIBUTION
- LEVERAGE_CASCADE
- LIQUIDITY_SHOCK
- COUNTERPARTY_CONTAGION
- PEG_FAILURE
- REGULATORY_EVENT
- PROTOCOL_EVENT
- ADOPTION_EVENT

Labels are hypotheses/state descriptors, not conclusions.

## Promotion sequence
`REGISTERED → EXECUTABLE → SOURCE_COMPLETE → REPLAYED → CALIBRATION_SAMPLE`

Promotion requires evidence gates; it is never manually inferred from price alone.
